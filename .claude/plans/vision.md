# Parallel arabic is a language learning app for arabic and its dialects, currently Egyptian, Levantine, Fusha, and Darija

## The big picture
The big picture is enabling users to generate learning materials on demand using words that they are currently studying. Allowing them to see and interact with words in various different contexts and situations, as oppossed to one off flashcards. We want to enable users to create a massive "ocean" of words that slowly expands overtime, which will signify their progress in language acquisition as opposed to arbritary levels like a1, a2, b1 etc which no app can realistically judge on behalf of the user.

## Lessons
Users can go through lessons and be introduced to new words, grammer and sentences

## Review
Users can review saved words via spaced repition

## Stories
Users can generate stories using their saved words to view them in natural formats

## Sentences
Users can create practice sentences using their saved words and practice writing the arabic script which really exercises the brain
