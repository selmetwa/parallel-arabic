The imperative ("do it!") is built from the jussive present. Fusha has five forms: to a man, a woman, two people, a group of men and a group of women.

## Making the imperative

Take the jussive for **anta**, **anti** and so on, drop the **ta-** prefix, and add a helping vowel at the front if the stem starts with two consonants. The helping vowel is **u-** when the stem vowel is **u**, and **i-** otherwise.

| Verb | To a man | To a woman | To two | To a group (m) | To a group (f) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| write (taktubu) | اكتبْ uktub | اكتبي uktubii | اكتبا uktubaa | اكتبوا uktubuu | اكتبْنَ uktubna |
| drink (tashrabu) | اشربْ ishrab | اشربي ishrabii | اشربا ishrabaa | اشربوا ishrabuu | اشربْنَ ishrabna |
| sit (tajlisu) | اجلسْ ijlis | اجلسي ijlisii | اجلسا ijlisaa | اجلسوا ijlisuu | اجلسْنَ ijlisna |

```ex
اكتبْ اسمَكَ هنا | uktub ismaka hunaa | Write your name here. (to a man)
انظري إلى هذا | unzurii ilaa haadha | Look at this. (to a woman)
انتظروا قليلاً | intaziruu qaliilan | Wait a little. (to a group)
```

## Derived forms and weak verbs

Form II and III stems start with a consonant + vowel, so no helping vowel is needed:

```ex
علّمْ / ساعدْ | 3allim / saa3id | teach! / help!
```

Hollow verbs shorten their long vowel in the masculine singular: **qul** (say!), **qulii** (to a woman).

## Form IV

Form IV imperatives start with **a-**: **a3ti** (give!), **arsil** (send!), as in **arsil ar-risaalata** (send the letter).

## Don't…

A negative command uses **laa** + the jussive:

```ex
لا تكتبْ | laa taktub | Don't write. (to a man)
لا تنسَيْ | laa tansay | Don't forget. (to a woman)
لا تتأخروا | laa tata2akhkharuu | Don't be late. (to a group)
```

> To soften a command, add **min fadlika** / **min fadliki** (please, to a man / woman), or **law sama7ta** (if you would).
