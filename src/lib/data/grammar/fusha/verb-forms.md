Most Arabic words grow from a **root** of three consonants that carries a core meaning. A verb **form** is a fixed pattern laid over the root, and each pattern nudges the meaning in a predictable direction. Fusha uses all ten common forms.

## Roots

The root **k-t-b** is about writing:

```ex
كتب | kataba | he wrote
كتاب | kitaab | book
مكتبة | maktaba | library, bookshop
كاتب | kaatib | writer
مكتوب | maktuub | written
```

## The ten forms

Grammarians number the patterns with Roman numerals and use **f-3-l** as a placeholder root.

| Form | Past | Present | Masdar (verbal noun) | Typical meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | fa3ala | yaf3alu | (varies) | the basic meaning | kataba (write) |
| II | fa33ala | yufa33ilu | taf3iil | make someone do it; intensive | darrasa (teach) |
| III | faa3ala | yufaa3ilu | mufaa3ala | do it with/to someone | saa3ada (help) |
| IV | af3ala | yuf3ilu | if3aal | make someone do it | akhraja (take out) |
| V | tafa33ala | yatafa33alu | tafa33ul | reflexive of II | ta3allama (learn) |
| VI | tafaa3ala | yatafaa3alu | tafaa3ul | do it to each other | ta3aawana (cooperate) |
| VII | infa3ala | yanfa3ilu | infi3aal | passive; get done | inkasara (break, intr.) |
| VIII | ifta3ala | yafta3ilu | ifti3aal | self-directed | ijtama3a (meet, gather) |
| IX | if3alla | yaf3allu | if3ilaal | colours, defects | i7marra (turn red) |
| X | istaf3ala | yastaf3ilu | istif3aal | seek, consider | istakhdama (use) |

Notice that Forms II, III and IV have **yu-** in the present, while the others have **ya-**.

## Families from one root

```ex
علِم / علّم / تعلّم / استعلم | 3alima / 3allama / ta3allama / ista3lama | know / teach / learn / inquire
خرج / أخرج / استخرج | kharaja / akhraja / istakhraja | go out / take out / extract
```

## The masdar

Each form has a fixed verbal noun ("the -ing"), except Form I, whose masdars have to be learned: **ta3liim** (teaching, from II), **musaa3ada** (help, from III), **istikhdaam** (use, from X).
