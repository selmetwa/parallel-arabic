In Fusha, "this" and "that" usually come **before** the noun, and the noun takes **al-** ("the").

## The forms

| | This | That |
| --- | --- | --- |
| masculine | هذا haadha | ذلك dhaalika |
| feminine | هذه haadhihi | تلك tilka |
| plural (people) | هؤلاء haa2ulaa2i | أولئك ulaa2ika |

There are also dual forms (**haadhaani**, **haataani**), used for exactly two.

## Before the noun with al-: "this house"

```ex
هذا البيتُ كبيرٌ | haadha al-baytu kabiirun | This house is big.
هذه المدينةُ جميلةٌ | haadhihi al-madiinatu jamiilatun | This city is beautiful.
ذلك الرجلُ | dhaalika ar-rajulu | that man
```

## Without al-: "this is…"

Leave **al-** off the noun and you've made a sentence:

```ex
هذا بيتٌ | haadha baytun | This is a house.
هذه سيارةٌ جديدةٌ | haadhihi sayyaaratun jadiidatun | This is a new car.
```

## "This is the…"

To say "this is the book", add the pronoun **huwa** / **hiya** between:

```ex
هذا هو الكتابُ | haadha huwa al-kitaabu | This is the book.
```

> Plurals of things are treated as feminine singular, so they take **haadhihi**: **haadhihi al-kutubu** (these books).
