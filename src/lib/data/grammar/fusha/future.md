To talk about the future in Fusha, put **sa-** (near future) or **sawfa** (more distant or emphatic) in front of the present.

## sa- + present

**sa-** is joined to the verb:

| Person | Present | Future |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ana | aktubu | سأكتبُ sa-aktubu |
| anta | taktubu | ستكتبُ sa-taktubu |
| huwa | yaktubu | سيكتبُ sa-yaktubu |
| na7nu | naktubu | سنكتبُ sa-naktubu |
| hum | yaktubuuna | سيكتبون sa-yaktubuuna |

```ex
سأسافرُ غداً | sa-usaafiru ghadan | I will travel tomorrow.
هل ستحضرُ الحفلةَ؟ | hal sa-ta7duru al-7aflata? | Will you attend the party?
```

## sawfa

**sawfa** is a separate word with the same meaning. It sounds more formal or more certain:

```ex
سوف نرى | sawfa naraa | We shall see.
```

## Negative future: lan

To say "will not", use **lan** + the subjunctive (the **-a** ending), and drop **sa-**:

```ex
لن أذهبَ | lan adhhaba | I will not go.
لن نتأخرَ | lan nata2akhkhara | We will not be late.
```

> **lan** is strong: it often means "never will". Don't combine it with **sa-**.
