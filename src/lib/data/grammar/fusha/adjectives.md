Adjectives come **after** the noun and match it in gender, number, "the" and case.

## Matching gender

```ex
ولدٌ كبيرٌ | waladun kabiirun | a big boy
بنتٌ كبيرةٌ | bintun kabiiratun | a big girl
```

## Matching "the": description vs sentence

If the noun has **al-**, the adjective takes **al-** too. Leave it off and you've made a sentence instead:

```ex
الولدُ الكبيرُ | al-waladu al-kabiiru | the big boy
الولدُ كبيرٌ | al-waladu kabiirun | The boy is big.
```

## Matching number

People take plural adjectives:

```ex
أولادٌ كبارٌ | awlaadun kibaarun | big boys
مُهندسون ماهرون | muhandisuuna maahiruuna | skilled engineers
```

Plurals of **things** take the **feminine singular**:

```ex
سياراتٌ جديدةٌ | sayyaaraatun jadiidatun | new cars
كتبٌ مفيدةٌ | kutubun mufiidatun | useful books
```

## Matching case

The adjective copies the noun's case ending:

```ex
في البيتِ الكبيرِ | fii al-bayti al-kabiiri | in the big house
```

## Nationality adjectives (nisba)

Add **-iyy** to a place name; the feminine is **-iyya**:

```ex
مصريّ / مصريّة / مصريّون | misriyy / misriyya / misriyyuun | Egyptian (m / f / pl)
```
