Fusha has three common words for "if": **idhaa** and **in** for real conditions, and **law** for imagined ones.

## Real conditions: idhaa + past

**idhaa** is the most common "if". It's followed by the **past** tense, even for the future:

```ex
إذا جاءتْ، سأخبرُها | idhaa jaa2at, sa-ukhbiruhaa | If she comes, I will tell her.
إذا كان عندك وقتٌ، تعالَ | idhaa kaana 3indaka waqtun, ta3aala | If you have time, come.
```

When the second half isn't a simple past verb (a future, a command, a noun sentence), it starts with **fa-**:

```ex
إذا درستَ فستنجحُ | idhaa darasta fa-sa-tanja7u | If you study, you will succeed.
```

## in: formal and literary

**in** is more formal. It takes the past or the jussive in both halves:

```ex
إنْ تدرسْ تنجحْ | in tadrus tanja7 | If you study, you will succeed.
```

## Imagined conditions: law … la-

For unreal or past conditions, use **law** + past, and start the result with **la-**:

```ex
لو كنتُ أعرفُ لقلتُ لك | law kuntu a3rifu la-qultu laka | If I had known, I would have told you.
لو كان معي مالٌ لاشتريتُه | law kaana ma3ii maalun la-shtaraytuhu | If I had had money, I would have bought it.
```

## Useful combinations

```ex
حتى لو | 7attaa law | even if
إلا إذا | illaa idhaa | unless
لولا | lawlaa | if it weren't for
```
