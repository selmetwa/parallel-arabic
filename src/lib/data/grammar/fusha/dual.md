Fusha has a full dual for exactly two of anything. Nouns, adjectives, pronouns and verbs all have dual forms.

## Nouns: -aani / -ayni

The ending is **-aani** for the subject and **-ayni** otherwise:

```ex
كتابان | kitaabaani | two books (subject)
قرأتُ كتابين | qara2tu kitaabayni | I read two books.
```

Nouns ending in **ة** keep the **t**:

```ex
سيارتان | sayyaarataani | two cars
مدينتين | madiinatayni | two cities (object)
```

## No number needed

The dual already means "two". Add **ithnaani** / **ithnataani** only for emphasis:

```ex
عندي أخوان | 3indii akhawaani | I have two brothers.
```

## Adjectives and verbs agree

```ex
الطالبانِ مُجتهدانِ | at-taalibaani mujtahidaani | The two students are hard-working.
هما يدرسانِ | huma yadrusaani | They (two) study.
```

## The -ni drops before an owner

When a dual noun is followed by an owner or a possessive ending, the final **-ni** drops:

```ex
يدا الطفلِ | yadaa at-tifli | the child's two hands
عيناها | 3aynaahaa | her (two) eyes
```
