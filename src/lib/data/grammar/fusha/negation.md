Fusha has a different word for "not" for each tense, plus **laysa** for sentences without a verb.

## The negatives at a glance

| Meaning | Word | Verb form | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| doesn't (present) | لا laa | present (indicative) | laa aktubu: I don't write |
| didn't (past) | لم lam | present (jussive) | lam aktub: I didn't write |
| didn't (past, less formal) | ما maa | past | maa katabtu: I didn't write |
| won't (future) | لن lan | present (subjunctive) | lan aktuba: I won't write |
| isn't (no verb) | ليس laysa | (agrees with subject) | lastu muta3aban: I'm not tired |

**lam** with the jussive is the standard way to negate the past. Notice that it uses a *present* form to talk about the past.

```ex
لا أعرفُ | laa a3rifu | I don't know.
لم يأتِ أحدٌ | lam ya2ti a7adun | Nobody came.
لن أنسى | lan ansaa | I won't forget.
```

## laysa: "is not"

**laysa** conjugates like a past verb, and its description takes the accusative (**-an**):

| Person | Form |
| --- | --- |
| ana | لستُ lastu |
| anta / anti | لستَ lasta / لستِ lasti |
| huwa / hiya | ليس laysa / ليست laysat |
| na7nu | لسنا lasnaa |
| hum | ليسوا laysuu |

```ex
لستُ متعباً | lastu muta3aban | I'm not tired.
هذا ليس بيتي | haadha laysa baytii | This isn't my house.
```

## Negative commands

Use **laa** + the jussive:

```ex
لا تقلقْ | laa taqlaq | Don't worry. (to a man)
لا تقلقي | laa taqlaqii | Don't worry. (to a woman)
```

## "There is no…"

```ex
لا مشكلةَ | laa mushkilata | There's no problem.
ليس عندي وقتٌ | laysa 3indii waqtun | I don't have time.
```
