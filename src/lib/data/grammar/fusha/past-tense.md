The past tense (the "perfect") is one stem with an ending for each person.

## Endings

Here is **kataba** (he wrote):

| Person | Ending | Form |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ana (I) | -tu | katabtu |
| anta (you m) | -ta | katabta |
| anti (you f) | -ti | katabti |
| huwa (he) | -a | kataba |
| hiya (she) | -at | katabat |
| antumaa (you two) | -tumaa | katabtumaa |
| humaa (they two, m) | -aa | katabaa |
| na7nu (we) | -naa | katabnaa |
| antum (you pl m) | -tum | katabtum |
| antunna (you pl f) | -tunna | katabtunna |
| hum (they m) | -uu | katabuu |
| hunna (they f) | -na | katabna |

"He" (**kataba**) is the form dictionaries list.

```ex
كتبتُ الرسالةَ أمسِ | katabtu ar-risaalata amsi | I wrote the letter yesterday.
خرجتْ مبكراً | kharajat mubakkiran | She went out early.
شربنا الشايَ | sharibnaa ash-shaaya | We drank tea.
```

## Three vowel patterns

Form I verbs have a past stem in **fa3ala**, **fa3ila** or **fa3ula**:

```ex
كتب / شرب / كبر | kataba / shariba / kabura | he wrote / he drank / he grew big
```

## Verbs that change shape

Verbs with **w** or **y** in the root change shape before the endings:

```ex
قال / قلتُ | qaala / qultu | he said / I said
مشى / مشيتُ | mashaa / mashaytu | he walked / I walked
ظنّ / ظننتُ | zanna / zanantu | he thought / I thought
```

See [verb classes](/grammar/fusha/verb-classes) for why, and the [verb tables](/fusha/conjugations) for full conjugations.

> To make the past negative, use **lam** + the jussive present: **lam aktub** (I didn't write). See [saying "not"](/grammar/fusha/negation).
