Fusha question words go at the **start** of the question.

## The question words

| English | Fusha | Transliteration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| what (before a verb) | ماذا | maadha |
| what (before a noun) | ما | maa |
| where | أين | ayna |
| when | متى | mataa |
| why | لماذا | limaadha |
| how | كيف | kayfa |
| who | مَن | man |
| how many | كم | kam |
| which | أيّ | ayy |

## Examples

```ex
ماذا تفعلُ؟ | maadha taf3alu? | What are you (m) doing?
ما اسمُكِ؟ | maa ismuki? | What is your (f) name?
أين تسكنُ؟ | ayna taskunu? | Where do you (m) live?
متى الحفلةُ؟ | mataa al-7aflatu? | When is the party?
لماذا تأخرتَ؟ | limaadha ta2akhkharta? | Why were you (m) late?
كيف حالُكَ؟ | kayfa 7aaluka? | How are you (m)?
مَن هذا؟ | man haadha? | Who is this?
```

## kam + accusative singular

```ex
كم كتاباً قرأتَ؟ | kam kitaaban qara2ta? | How many books did you read?
بكم هذا؟ | bikam haadha? | How much is this?
```

## Yes/no questions: hal and a-

Start a yes/no question with **hal**, or attach **a-**:

```ex
هل أنتَ مصريّ؟ | hal anta misriyy? | Are you Egyptian?
أتتكلّمُ العربيةَ؟ | a-tatakallamu al-3arabiyya? | Do you speak Arabic?
```

> **ayna** combines with prepositions: **min ayna?** (from where?), **ilaa ayna?** (where to?).
