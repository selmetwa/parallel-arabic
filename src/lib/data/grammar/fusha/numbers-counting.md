If you've seen "I want 30 apple" in an example and wondered whether it was a mistake: it isn't. In Arabic, the form of the noun depends on the number in front of it, and from 11 upwards the noun is **singular**.

## The rule

| Number | Noun form | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | singular (+ **waa7id** for emphasis) | كتابٌ kitaabun |
| 2 | dual | كتابان kitaabaani |
| 3 to 10 | genitive plural | ثلاثةُ كتبٍ thalaathatu kutubin |
| 11 to 99 | accusative singular (**-an**) | أحدَ عشرَ كتاباً a7ada 3ashara kitaaban |
| 100, 1,000… | genitive singular (**-in**) | مئةُ كتابٍ mi2atu kitaabin |

So 3 to 10 is the only range that uses the plural. From 11 on the noun stays singular, with an **-an** ending up to 99 and an **-in** ending after hundreds and thousands.

```ex
أريدُ ثلاثين تفاحةً | uriidu thalaathiina tuffaa7atan | I want thirty apples.
معه مئةُ دولارٍ | ma3ahu mi2atu duulaarin | He has a hundred dollars.
عشرون سنةً | 3ishruuna sanatan | twenty years
```

## 11 to 19

Both parts are fixed with a final **-a**. For 13 to 19, the units take the opposite gender to the noun, and **3ashara** matches it:

| | With a masculine noun | With a feminine noun |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 11 | أحد عشر a7ada 3ashara | إحدى عشرة i7daa 3ashrata |
| 12 | اثنا عشر ithnaa 3ashara | اثنتا عشرة ithnataa 3ashrata |
| 13 | ثلاثة عشر thalaathata 3ashara | ثلاث عشرة thalaatha 3ashrata |
| 15 | خمسة عشر khamsata 3ashara | خمس عشرة khamsa 3ashrata |

## Tens, hundreds, thousands

| Number | Fusha | Number | Fusha |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 20 | عشرون 3ishruuna | 60 | ستّون sittuuna |
| 30 | ثلاثون thalaathuuna | 70 | سبعون sab3uuna |
| 40 | أربعون arba3uuna | 80 | ثمانون thamaanuuna |
| 50 | خمسون khamsuuna | 90 | تسعون tis3uuna |
| 100 | مئة mi2a | 1,000 | ألف alf |

The tens end in **-uuna** as a subject and **-iina** otherwise: **ra2aytu 3ishriina taaliban** (I saw twenty students).

## Numbers like 25: units first

```ex
خمسةٌ وعشرون | khamsatun wa 3ishruuna | twenty-five
```

> "How many?" is **kam**, and it takes an accusative singular: **kam kitaaban?** (how many books?).
