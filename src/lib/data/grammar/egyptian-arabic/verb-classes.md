Some verbs follow the conjugation patterns exactly. Others change shape, because their root has a **weak letter**: **w**, **y** or a hamza (**ء**). The verb's class tells you which kind it is.

## Sound verbs

Three ordinary consonants. These follow the patterns with no surprises.

```ex
كتب / كتبت / بيكتب | katab / katabt / biyiktib | he wrote / I wrote / he writes
```

## Hollow verbs: w or y in the middle

The middle letter shows up as a long vowel. It **shortens** when the ending starts with a consonant (I, you, we).

| | Past (he) | Past (I) | Present (he) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| see | شاف shaaf | شفت shuft | بيشوف biyshuuf |
| sleep | نام naam | نمت nimt | بينام biynaam |
| say | قال 2aal | قلت 2ult | بيقول biy2uul |

## Defective verbs: w or y at the end

The verb ends in a vowel, which merges with the endings:

| | Past (he) | Past (I) | Present (he) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| forget | نسي nisi | نسيت nisiit | بينسى biyinsa |
| walk | مشي mishi | مشيت mishiit | بيمشي biyimshi |
| read | قرا 2ara | قريت 2areet | بيقرا biyi2ra |

## Doubled verbs: the same letter twice

The second and third root letters are the same. Before endings starting with a consonant, they add **-ee-**:

```ex
حب / حبيت / بيحب | 7abb / 7abbeet / biy7ibb | he loved / I loved / he loves
```

## Irregular verbs

A few very common verbs don't fit any pattern. Learn their full tables:

```ex
جه / جيت / بييجي | geh / geet / biyiigi | he came / I came / he comes
أكل / بياكل | akal / biyaakul | he ate / he eats
أخد / باخد | akhad / baakhud | he took / I take
```

> Each verb in the [verb tables](/egyptian-arabic/conjugations) is labelled with its class, so you can compare it with others like it.
