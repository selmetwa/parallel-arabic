The imperative ("do it!") is built from the present stem. There are three forms: to a man, to a woman and to a group.

## Making the imperative

Take the bare present for **inta**, **inti** or **intu**, drop the **ti-** prefix, and add **i-** at the front if the stem starts with two consonants.

| Verb | To a man | To a woman | To a group |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| write (tiktib) | iktib | iktibi | iktibu |
| drink (tishrab) | ishrab | ishrabi | ishrabu |
| open (tifta7) | ifta7 | ifta7i | ifta7u |
| see (tishuuf) | shuuf | shuufi | shuufu |
| wait (tistanna) | istanna | istanni | istannu |

Stems that start with a single consonant (like **-shuuf**) don't need the extra **i-**.

```ex
اكتب اسمك هنا | iktib ismak hina | Write your name here. (to a man)
شوفي ده | shuufi da | Look at this. (to a woman)
استنوا شوية | istannu shwayya | Wait a bit. (to a group)
```

## Common irregular commands

Some of the most used commands are irregular:

| Meaning | To a man | To a woman | To a group |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| come | تعالى ta3aala | تعالي ta3aali | تعالوا ta3aalu |
| give (me) | هات haat | هاتي haati | هاتوا haatu |
| take | خد khud | خدي khudi | خدوا khudu |
| eat | كل kul | كلي kuli | كلوا kulu |

## Don't…

A negative command uses the full present form with **ma-…-sh**:

```ex
ما تكتبش | ma tiktibsh | Don't write. (to a man)
ما تنسيش | ma tinsiish | Don't forget. (to a woman)
ما تتأخروش | ma tit2akhkharuush | Don't be late. (to a group)
```

> To soften a command, add **min fadlak** / **min fadlik** (please, to a man / woman) or start with **law sama7t** (excuse me).
