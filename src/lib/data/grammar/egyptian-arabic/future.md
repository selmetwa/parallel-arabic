To talk about the future in Egyptian, put **ha-** in front of the bare present (the form without **bi-**).

## ha- + bare present

| Person | Present | Future |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ana | aktib | haktib |
| inta | tiktib | hatiktib |
| inti | tiktibi | hatiktibi |
| huwwa | yiktib | hayiktib |
| hiyya | tiktib | hatiktib |
| i7na | niktib | haniktib |
| intu | tiktibu | hatiktibu |
| humma | yiktibu | hayiktibu |

It's written **هـ** joined to the verb (you'll also see **حـ**).

```ex
هسافر بكرة | hasaafir bukra | I'll travel tomorrow.
هتيجي الحفلة؟ | hatiigi il-7afla? | Will you come to the party?
هيتأخروا شوية | hayit2akhkharu shwayya | They'll be a little late.
```

## "Going to" with raayi7

For a plan, you can also use **raayi7** (going), which agrees like an adjective:

```ex
أنا رايح أذاكر | ana raayi7 azaakir | I'm going to study.
هي رايحة تشتري هدوم | hiyya ray7a tishtiri huduum | She's going to buy clothes.
```

## Negative future

Use **mish** before the future verb:

```ex
مش هروح | mish haruu7 | I won't go.
مش هنتأخر | mish hanit2akhkhar | We won't be late.
```

> In speech, **ha-** before **a** often merges: **ha + aktib** → **haktib**.
