Egyptian has two main ways to say "not": **ma-…-sh**, which wraps around a verb, and **mish**, which goes in front of everything else.

## ma-…-sh: verbs in the past and present

Put **ma-** in front of the verb and **-sh** on the end. If the verb ends in a vowel, the vowel lengthens.

```ex
ما كتبتش | ma katabtish | I didn't write.
ما بكتبش | ma baktibsh | I don't write.
ما شافوش | ma shafuush | They didn't see.
```

**ma-…-sh** also works with **3and** (have) and **fii** (there is):

```ex
ما عنديش وقت | ma3andiish wa2t | I don't have time.
مفيش مشكلة | mafiish mushkila | There's no problem.
```

## mish: everything else

Use **mish** for adjectives, nouns, participles and the future:

```ex
أنا مش تعبان | ana mish ta3baan | I'm not tired.
ده مش بيتي | da mish beeti | This isn't my house.
مش عايز | mish 3aayiz | I don't want (to).
مش هروح | mish haruu7 | I won't go.
```

## Negative commands

Commands use **ma-** + the bare present + **-sh**:

```ex
ما تقلقش | ma ti2la2sh | Don't worry. (to a man)
ما تقلقيش | ma ti2la2iish | Don't worry. (to a woman)
```

## Never, nothing, nobody

```ex
عمري ما رحت | 3umri ma ru7t | I've never been.
ما حدش جه | ma7addish geh | Nobody came.
ما قلتش حاجة | ma 2ultish 7aaga | I didn't say anything.
```

> A quick test: if you could put **bi-** or a past ending on it, it's a verb and takes **ma-…-sh**. Otherwise use **mish**.
