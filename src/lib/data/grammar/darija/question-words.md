Darija question words usually go at the **start** of the question, as in English.

## The question words

| English | Darija | Transliteration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| what | شنو / آش | shnu / ash |
| where | فين | fin |
| when | إمتى | imta |
| why | علاش | 3lash |
| how | كيفاش | kifash |
| who | شكون | shkun |
| how much / how many | شحال | sh7al |
| which | أشمن | ashmen |

## Examples

```ex
شنو كدير؟ | shnu kadir? | What are you doing?
فين ساكن؟ | fin saken? | Where do you (m) live?
إمتى الحفلة؟ | imta l-7efla? | When is the party?
علاش تعطلتي؟ | 3lash t3ettelti? | Why were you late?
كيفاش داير؟ | kifash dayer? | How are you (m)?
شكون هادا؟ | shkun hada? | Who's that?
```

## How much, what time

```ex
بشحال هادا؟ | b-sh7al hada? | How much is this?
شحال فالساعة؟ | sh7al f s-sa3a? | What time is it?
```

## Yes/no questions: wash

Darija often starts a yes/no question with **wash**:

```ex
واش نتا مغربي؟ | wash nta meghribi? | Are you Moroccan?
واش كتهضر العربية؟ | wash kathder l-3arabiya? | Do you speak Arabic?
```

> **fin** combines with prepositions: **mnin?** (from where?), **l-fin?** (where to?).
