Adjectives come **after** the noun and match it in gender, number and "the".

## Matching gender

```ex
ولد كبير | weld kbir | a big boy
بنت كبيرة | bent kbira | a big girl
```

## Matching "the": description vs sentence

If the noun has **l-**, the adjective takes **l-** too. Leave it off and you've made a sentence instead.

```ex
الولد الكبير | l-weld l-kbir | the big boy
الولد كبير | l-weld kbir | The boy is big.
```

## Matching number

People take plural adjectives, often broken plurals:

```ex
ولاد كبار | wlad kbar | big children
ناس مزيانين | nas mezyanin | good people
```

Plurals of things usually take the plural or the feminine singular:

```ex
طوموبيلات جداد | tomobilat jdad | new cars
```

## Nationality adjectives (-i)

```ex
مغربي / مغربية / مغاربة | meghribi / meghribiya / mgharba | Moroccan (m / f / pl)
```

## Colours

| Masculine | Feminine | Plural | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| حمر 7mer | حمرا 7emra | حومر 7umer | red |
| زرق zreq | زرقا zerqa | زورق zureq | blue |
| بيض byed | بيضا bida | بيوض biyed | white |
| كحل k7el | كحلا ke7la | كوحل ku7el | black |

> **mezyan** (good) and **zwin** (nice, pretty) are the two adjectives you'll hear most: **mezyan / mezyana / mezyanin**, **zwin / zwina / zwinin**.
