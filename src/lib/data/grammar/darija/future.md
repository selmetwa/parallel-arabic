To talk about the future in Darija, put **ghadi** in front of the bare present (the form without **ka-**).

## ghadi + bare present

| Person | Present | Future |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ana | nkteb | ghadi nkteb |
| nta | tkteb | ghadi tkteb |
| nti | tketbi | ghadi tketbi |
| huwa | ykteb | ghadi ykteb |
| hiya | tkteb | ghadi tkteb |
| 7na | nketbu | ghadi nketbu |
| ntuma | tketbu | ghadi tketbu |
| huma | yketbu | ghadi yketbu |

In fast speech **ghadi** shortens to **gha-**: **ghankteb** (I'll write).

```ex
غادي نسافر غدا | ghadi nsafer ghedda | I'll travel tomorrow.
واش غادي تجي للحفلة؟ | wash ghadi tji l-l-7efla? | Will you come to the party?
```

**ghadi** can also agree with the subject: **ghadya** (f), **ghadyin** (pl).

## Negative future

Use **ma ghadish**:

```ex
ما غاديش نمشي | ma ghadish nmshi | I won't go.
```
