Darija uses a special dual ending, **-ayn**, but only with a small set of words, mostly for time and measures. For everything else it uses **jouj** ("two").

## The dual ending -ayn

```ex
يومين | yumayn | two days
عامين | 3amayn | two years
شهرين | shhrayn | two months
مرتين | merrtayn | twice
```

## jouj for everything else

For most nouns, say **jouj** followed by the plural. You'll often hear **d** in between when the noun has **l-**:

```ex
جوج كتوب | jouj ktub | two books
جوج د الولاد | jouj d l-wlad | two children
عندي جوج خوت | 3ndi jouj khut | I have two brothers.
```

> **jouj** is the Moroccan word for "two" when counting things. When counting out loud, people also say **jouj**: **wa7ed, jouj, tlata…**
