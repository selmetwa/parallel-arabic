The imperative ("do it!") is built from the present stem. There are three forms: to a man, to a woman and to a group.

## Making the imperative

Take the bare present for **nta**, **nti** or **ntuma** and drop the **t-** prefix:

| Verb | To a man | To a woman | To a group |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| write (tkteb) | kteb | ketbi | ketbu |
| drink (teshreb) | shreb | sherbi | sherbu |
| open (t7ell) | 7ell | 7elli | 7ellu |
| see (tshuf) | shuf | shufi | shufu |
| wait (tsenna) | tsenna | tsennay | tsennaw |

```ex
كتب سميتك هنا | kteb smitek hna | Write your name here. (to a man)
شوفي هادي | shufi hadi | Look at this. (to a woman)
تسناو شوية | tsennaw shwiya | Wait a bit. (to a group)
```

## Common irregular commands

| Meaning | To a man | To a woman | To a group |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| come | أجي aji | أجي aji | أجيو ajiw |
| give (me) | عطيني 3tini | عطيني 3tini | عطيوني 3tiwni |
| take | خود khud | خودي khudi | خودو khudu |
| eat | كول kul | كولي kuli | كولو kulu |

## Don't…

A negative command uses **ma** + the full present + **-sh**:

```ex
ما تكتبش | ma tktebsh | Don't write. (to a man)
ما تنسايش | ma tnsaysh | Don't forget. (to a woman)
```

> To soften a command, add **3afak** (please): **3tini l-ma 3afak** (give me the water, please).
