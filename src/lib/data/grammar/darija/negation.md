Darija has two main ways to say "not": **ma-…-sh**, which wraps around a verb, and **mashi**, which goes in front of everything else.

## ma-…-sh: verbs

Put **ma** in front of the verb and **-sh** on the end:

```ex
ما كتبتش | ma ktebtsh | I didn't write.
ما كنعرفش | ma kan3refsh | I don't know.
ما شافوهش | ma shafuhsh | They didn't see him.
```

**ma-…-sh** also works with **3nd** (have) and **kayn** (there is):

```ex
ما عنديش الوقت | ma 3ndish l-weqt | I don't have time.
ما كاينش مشكل | ma kaynsh mushkil | There's no problem.
```

## mashi: everything else

Use **mashi** for adjectives, nouns and participles:

```ex
أنا ماشي عيان | ana mashi 3eyyan | I'm not tired.
هادي ماشي داري | hadi mashi dari | This isn't my house.
```

## Negative commands

Commands use **ma** + the bare present + **-sh**:

```ex
ما تخافش | ma tkhafsh | Don't be afraid. (to a man)
ما تخافيش | ma tkhafish | Don't be afraid. (to a woman)
```

## Never, nothing, nobody

```ex
عمري ما مشيت | 3mmri ma mshit | I've never been.
حتى واحد ما جا | 7tta wa7ed ma ja | Nobody came.
ما قلت والو | ma qolt walu | I didn't say anything.
```

When a sentence has **walu** (nothing), **7tta wa7ed** (nobody) or **3mmri** (never), the **-sh** is dropped.
