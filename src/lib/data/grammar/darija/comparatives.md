Darija has one pattern for both "bigger" and "the biggest". Where other dialects say **akbar**, Darija drops the **a-** and says **kber**. It doesn't change for gender or number.

## Making the comparative

| Adjective | Comparative | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| كبير kbir | كبر kber | bigger |
| صغير sghir | صغر sgher | smaller |
| زوين zwin | زين zyen | nicer |
| مزيان mezyan | حسن 7sen | better |
| غالي ghali | غلى ghla | more expensive |
| رخيص rkhis | رخص rkhes | cheaper |
| بعيد b3id | بعد b3ed | further |

**mezyan** (good) takes **7sen**, from a different root.

## "Than" is men

```ex
كازا كبر من الرباط | kaza kber men r-rbat | Casablanca is bigger than Rabat.
هي طول منو | hiya twel mennu | She's taller than him.
هادا رخص من داك | hada rkhes men dak | This one is cheaper than that one.
```

## The superlative

Put the comparative **before** a noun, or use it with **l-**:

```ex
كبر مدينة | kber mdina | the biggest city
هو الحسن | huwa l-7sen | He's the best.
```

## Adjectives without the pattern

Longer adjectives use **ktar** (more) after the adjective:

```ex
فرحان كتر | fer7an ktar | happier
```
