Levantine has two main words for "not": **ma** for verbs, and **mish** (or **mu** in Syria) for everything else.

## ma: verbs

Put **ma** in front of a past or present verb:

```ex
ما كتبت | ma katabt | I didn't write.
ما بعرف | ma ba3ref | I don't know.
ما شافوه | ma shaafuu | They didn't see him.
```

**ma** also works with **3and** (have), **fi** (there is) and **badd-** (want):

```ex
ما عندي وقت | ma 3andi wa2t | I don't have time.
ما في مشكلة | ma fi mushkle | There's no problem.
ما بدّي | ma baddi | I don't want (to).
```

You'll sometimes hear an extra **-sh** in Palestine and southern Lebanon: **ma ba3rafsh**.

## mish / mu: everything else

Use **mish** (Lebanon, Palestine, Jordan) or **mu** (Syria) for adjectives, nouns and participles:

```ex
أنا مش تعبان | ana mish ta3baan | I'm not tired.
هادا مو بيتي | haada mu beiti | This isn't my house.
```

## Negative commands

Commands use **la** + the bare present:

```ex
لا تقلق | la ti2la2 | Don't worry. (to a man)
لا تقلقي | la ti2la2i | Don't worry. (to a woman)
```

## Never, nothing, nobody

```ex
عمري ما رحت | 3omri ma ri7t | I've never been.
ما حدا إجا | ma 7ada ija | Nobody came.
ما قلت شي | ma 2elt shi | I didn't say anything.
```
