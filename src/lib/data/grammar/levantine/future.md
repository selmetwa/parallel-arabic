To talk about the future in Levantine, put **ra7** in front of the bare present (the form without **b-**).

## ra7 + bare present

| Person | Present | Future |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ana | iktob | ra7 iktob |
| inta | tiktob | ra7 tiktob |
| inti | tiktbi | ra7 tiktbi |
| huwwe | yiktob | ra7 yiktob |
| hiyye | tiktob | ra7 tiktob |
| ni7na | niktob | ra7 niktob |
| intu | tiktbu | ra7 tiktbu |
| hinne | yiktbu | ra7 yiktbu |

```ex
رح سافر بكرا | ra7 saafer bukra | I'll travel tomorrow.
رح تيجي عالحفلة؟ | ra7 tiiji 3al-7afle? | Will you come to the party?
```

In Palestine and Jordan you'll also hear **7a-** joined to the verb: **7asaafer** (I'll travel).

## Negative future

Put **ma** in front of **ra7**:

```ex
ما رح روح | ma ra7 rou7 | I won't go.
ما رح نتأخر | ma ra7 nit2akhkhar | We won't be late.
```

> For a plan you've already made, the plain present also works: **bukra bsaafer** (I'm travelling tomorrow).
