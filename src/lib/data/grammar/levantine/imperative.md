The imperative ("do it!") is built from the present stem. There are three forms: to a man, to a woman and to a group.

## Making the imperative

Take the bare present for **inta**, **inti** or **intu**, drop the **t-** prefix, and add **i-** at the front if the stem starts with two consonants.

| Verb | To a man | To a woman | To a group |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| write (tiktob) | iktob | iktbi | iktbu |
| drink (tishrab) | ishrab | ishrabi | ishrabu |
| open (tifta7) | ifta7 | ifta7i | ifta7u |
| see (tshuuf) | shuuf | shuufi | shuufu |
| wait (tistanna) | istanna | istanni | istannu |

```ex
اكتب اسمك هون | iktob ismak hoon | Write your name here. (to a man)
شوفي هادا | shuufi haada | Look at this. (to a woman)
استنّوا شوي | istannu shway | Wait a bit. (to a group)
```

## Common irregular commands

| Meaning | To a man | To a woman | To a group |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| come | تعا ta3a | تعي ta3i | تعوا ta3u |
| give (me) | هات haat | هاتي haati | هاتوا haatu |
| take | خود khood | خدي khidi | خدوا khidu |
| eat | كول kool | كلي kili | كلوا kilu |

## Don't…

A negative command uses **la** + the full present:

```ex
لا تكتب | la tiktob | Don't write. (to a man)
لا تنسي | la tinsi | Don't forget. (to a woman)
لا تتأخروا | la tit2akhkharu | Don't be late. (to a group)
```

> To soften a command, add **min fadlak** / **min fadlik** (please, to a man / woman), or **iza bitriid** / **iza bitriidi** (if you'd like).
