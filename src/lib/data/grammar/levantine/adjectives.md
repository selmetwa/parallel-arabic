Adjectives come **after** the noun and match it in gender, number and "the".

## Matching gender

```ex
ولد كبير | walad kbiir | a big boy
بنت كبيرة | binit kbiire | a big girl
```

## Matching "the": description vs sentence

If the noun has **il-**, the adjective takes **il-** too. Leave it off the adjective and you've made a sentence instead.

```ex
الولد الكبير | il-walad il-kbiir | the big boy
الولد كبير | il-walad kbiir | The boy is big.
```

## Matching number

People take plural adjectives, often broken plurals:

```ex
ولاد كبار | wlaad kbaar | big children
ناس منيحين | naas mni7iin | nice people
```

Plurals of things usually take the feminine singular:

```ex
سيارات جديدة | sayyaaraat jdiide | new cars
```

## Nationality adjectives (-i)

```ex
لبناني / لبنانية / لبنانيين | libnaani / libnaaniyye / libnaaniyyiin | Lebanese (m / f / pl)
```
