# Saudi (Hijazi) — A1 — The Present Tense (base-a1-u18)
# Vocab gives the bare he-form; in sentences the habitual/ongoing present takes ب-: بَروح (I go), بِتروح (you go), بيروح (he goes), بِتروحي (you f. go). Hijazi: يِبغى = he wants.
