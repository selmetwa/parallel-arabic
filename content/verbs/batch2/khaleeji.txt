# Standard letters only; ج is said y and ق g in the transliteration.

## istakhabba
cit: تخبى/tkhabba
root: خ ب ي
class: defective
form: V
note: Gulf speakers say tkhabba for "hide (yourself)"; it ends in a vowel, so the past adds -eit.
past: تخبيت/tkhabbeit; تخبيت/tkhabbeit; تخبيتي/tkhabbeiti; تخبى/tkhabba; تخبت/tkhabbat; تخبينا/tkhabbeina; تخبيتوا/tkhabbeitaw; تخبوا/tkhabbaw
