## Insights & Metrics

**1. Weekly Active Users (WAU)**
- Formula: Number of unique users active at least once per week
- Measures: Consistent usage pattern development
- Why it matters: Regular weekly usage indicates habit formation better than daily spikes.

**2. Session Frequency**
- Formula: Average sessions per user per week
- Measures: How often users return to the product
- Why it matters: Frequent return visits are the core indicator of habit formation.

**3. Streak Completion Rate**
- Formula: (Users who maintain 7+ day streaks ÷ Users who start streaks) × 100
- Measures: User ability to maintain consistent behaviour
- Why it matters: Streaks are proven to reinforce habitual behaviour.

**4. Return Rate After 30+ Days**
- Formula: (Users who return after 30 days of inactivity ÷ Users who were inactive for 30+ days) × 100
- Measures: Long-term habit stickiness beyond short-term streaks
- Why it matters: Returning after long breaks signals meaningful product attachment.
