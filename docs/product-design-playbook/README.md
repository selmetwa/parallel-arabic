> Users don't just remember what your app does. They remember how it feels. This playbook gives you a founding-designer lens to map end-to-end flows that onboard, activate, retain, and monetise.

## How to use

Combine, remix, skip. It's a modular system: use what fits, drop what doesn't.

**a. Solve larger goals.** Pick a Product Strategy that addresses your goal, gather its play cards, and identify which plays could be used in your app. You now have a strategy for reaching the goal.

**b. Audit your product.** Pick a play card at random. Could it be used in your app? Use its "Make It Yours" card to personalise it. Do this three times to find quick wins.

**c. Explore "Pair with" routes.** Follow the interlinked plays, pick the ones that best support your vision, and link them into your own combinations.

**d. Move a metric.** Start with a metric you want to improve. Use the "Behind the Data" questions to uncover what drives it, match the findings to plays that address the drop-offs, then design, ship, measure, and loop.
