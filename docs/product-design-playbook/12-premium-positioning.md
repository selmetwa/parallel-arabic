## Insights & Metrics

**1. Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)**
- Formula: Total revenue ÷ Total number of users
- Measures: How much users are willing to pay for your product
- Why it matters: Core indicator of premium pricing power and perceived value.

**2. Price Sensitivity (Conversion by Price Tier)**
- Formula: Conversion rate at different price points during testing
- Measures: User willingness to pay premium prices
- Why it matters: Shows if users perceive enough value to justify premium pricing.

**3. Customer Satisfaction (Premium Features)**
- Formula: CSAT score specifically for premium/advanced features
- Measures: Whether premium features deliver expected value
- Why it matters: Premium users should be more satisfied to justify higher prices.

## Behind the Data

Decode how users perceive quality and where polish, detail, or friction is elevating or eroding the experience.

(The original playbook, both the PDF and the .md, prints these questions on the Habit Formation card. They're swapped back in this copy.)

**Premium Value Delivery**
- Do premium users have higher satisfaction than basic users?
- Which premium features drive highest usage and satisfaction?
- How does premium user retention compare to basic user retention?

**Pricing Power**
- How do conversion rates change at different price points?
- What's the optimal price increase that maintains conversion quality?
- Which user segments show lowest price sensitivity?

**Premium Experience**
- Do premium touches (animations, delays, polish) correlate with higher ARPU?
- How does premium onboarding affect long-term value perception?
- Which premium positioning elements most effectively justify higher prices?
